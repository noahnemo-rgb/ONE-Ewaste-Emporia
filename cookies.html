Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Our New Era (ONE) Church

1. Minimalist Cookie Philosophy

ONE E-Waste Emporia does not use intrusive third-party advertising cookies, behavioral trackers, or cross-site tracking pixels. We believe in privacy by design.

2. Cookies We Utilize

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Session tokens to maintain work order progress, form state, and authentication for admin technicians.
  • Security & Payment Verification (Stripe): Stripe sets cryptographic verification cookies (e.g., __stripe_mid, __stripe_sid) strictly to prevent fraudulent credit card transactions and ensure PCI-DSS compliance.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can configure your browser to reject cookies. Please note that disabling cookies may impede Stripe checkout flows or session persistence during work order entry.
