Terms of Service & Repair Agreement

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Sponsoring Body: Our New Era (ONE) Church

1. The Ministry & Social Enterprise Scope

ONE E-Waste Emporia is operated as a religious, charitable, and restorative social enterprise under the 501(c)(3) framework of Our New Era (ONE) Church. All proceeds, service fees, and voluntary donations support our Vocational Rehabilitation Guild for disabled individuals and re-entering citizens, computer recycling, and hardware grants for underprivileged families.

2. Diagnostics, Written Estimates & Flat-Rate Initial Fee

Diagnostic Fee Policy: A non-refundable initial flat-rate fee of $50.00 USD is required at the time of work order submission for all devices submitted under the "Diagnostic & Repair" service track.

Upon physical receipt of the device at our church workshop, our apprentice technicians and supervisory staff perform comprehensive hardware diagnostics. Within five (5) business days of intake, the customer will receive an itemized, written estimate detailing proposed repairs, required parts, labor, and expected completion timelines.

  • If Repair is Approved: The $50.00 diagnostic fee will be credited directly against the final approved repair invoice.
  • If Repair is Declined: The $50.00 diagnostic fee remains non-refundable, covering bench time, technical inspection, and testing materials.

3. Customer Work Order & Disposition Directives

For any device deemed unrepairable, or where repair costs are declined by the customer, the customer must select an explicit disposition option in their initial work order:

  • Disposition A (Return Unrepaired - Unwiped): Device is packaged and shipped back to the customer as-is. Customer is responsible for return postage and insurance.
  • Disposition B (Return Unrepaired - Sanitized): Prior to return shipment, storage media is wiped in compliance with NIST SP 800-88 sanitization standards. Customer covers return postage.
  • Disposition C (Charitable Donation & Upcycling): If repair is declined or impractical, ownership of the device is irrevocably transferred to ONE Church. The device is allocated to our Vocational Rehabilitation Guild for hands-on student teardown, component harvesting, or refurbishment and donation to a low-income family. A 501(c)(3) tax-deductible receipt will be provided.

4. Data Sanctuary: Recovery, Encryption & Delivery Protocol

For customers selecting the Data Recovery service track, recovered files are handled under strict forensic and privacy guidelines:

  • Delivery via Cloud Vault: Files are uploaded to an isolated, temporary encrypted vault. The customer receives a time-limited 14-day download URL. The decryption key / OTP is transmitted out-of-band via SMS or verified phone call. Decryption keys are never transmitted in unencrypted emails alongside links.
  • Delivery via Physical Media: If a physical USB drive or external SSD is selected, the media is formatted with AES-256 volume encryption. To prevent postal interception, decryption credentials are never enclosed within the physical shipment; they are dispatched digitally to the customer's verified contact upon confirmed postal delivery.
  • 30-Day Courtesy Safety Hold: To safeguard against lost parcels or failed downloads, the laboratory retains an encrypted master image on an isolated, air-gapped staging array for thirty (30) days following delivery. On Day 31 (or upon early customer written confirmation), the staging copy is cryptographically purged in compliance with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1.

5. Abandoned Property Clause

If a customer fails to respond to a written estimate, decline/approval notification, or unpaid return shipping invoice within sixty (60) calendar days of written electronic notice, the device shall be deemed abandoned. Title to abandoned devices automatically transfers to ONE Church for responsible recycling or donation.

6. Limitation of Liability & Data Responsibility

While our technicians adhere to the strictest anti-static, non-invasive forensic handling standards, customer acknowledges that electronic components may have pre-existing latent failures, micro-fractures, or irreversible media corruption. Customers are strongly encouraged to back up critical data prior to shipping. ONE Church's maximum aggregate liability for any device shall not exceed the lesser of the current fair market value of the hardware or $250.00 USD.

7. Governing Law

These terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction of incorporation of Our New Era Church, without regard to conflict of law principles.
