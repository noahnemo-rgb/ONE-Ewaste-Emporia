The Volunteer Blueprint

Discover the purpose, structure, schedule, and environment where your time and heart create enduring community transformation.

💜

1. WHY: The Agape Mission

Modern society routinely casts aside both obsolete electronics and human beings facing systemic barriers. We believe no soul and no creation is disposable.

  • • Restorative Justice: Mentor returning citizens transitioning home from prison sentences, providing supportive fellowship and technical coaching.
  • • Inclusion for the Disabled: Assist neurodivergent and physically disabled technicians working on adaptive, ergonomic benches.
  • • Digital Divide Eradication: Refurbish viable computers that are gifted directly to low-income students, foster youth, and isolated seniors.
  • • Sacred Earth Stewardship: Divert hazardous heavy metals and toxic chemicals from our landfills and waterways.
🔧

2. HOW: Pathways & Onboarding

You do not need to be an engineer to serve. We have structured pathways for all experience levels, from beginner sorters to master technicians.

  • • Hardware & Repair Track: Cleaning, disassembly, thermal paste replacement, RAM/SSD upgrades, and board triage.
  • • Software & Open-Source Lab: Flashing lightweight Linux OS, diagnostic testing, and sanitization verification.
  • • Apprentice Mentorship: Life-skills coaching, resume preparation, and tutoring apprentices toward their CompTIA A+ certification.
  • • Logistics, Intake & Packing: Receiving mailers, inventory barcode scanning, boxing, and dispatching pre-paid parcel kits.
🕒

3. WHEN: Shifts & Flexible Time

We honor your schedule. Whether you have 2 hours a month or want a dedicated weekly volunteer shift, there is a rhythm for you.

  • • Weekday Morning Guild: Tuesday & Thursday, 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (Active bench repairs, triage & testing).
  • • Wednesday Evening Fellowship & Solder Lab: 6:00 PM – 8:30 PM (Community dinner, apprentice tutoring & electronics repairs).
  • • E-Waste Saturday Drives: 1st & 3rd Saturday of each month, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Community drop-offs & bulk sorting).
  • • Remote Roles: Flexible hours (grant writing, curriculum design, virtual tutoring, website support).
📍

4. WHERE: Our Lab & Satellites

Serve on-site in our purpose-built technical sanctuary or remotely from the comfort of your home.

  • • The Tech Sanctuary Annex: Our New Era (ONE) Church Facility, 123 Sanctuary Way, Tech Annex Wing, Atlanta, GA 30301 (Grounded ESD benches, tool library, Class D safety containment).
  • • Church Parking Lot Drop-Off Satellites: Quarterly mobile pop-up intake drives throughout the regional community.
  • • The Virtual Lab: Contribute remotely through our online software stack, curriculum translation, and digital outreach.
