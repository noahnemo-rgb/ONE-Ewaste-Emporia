Privacy Policy & Handling of User Information

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Our New Era (ONE) Church

1. Our Sacred Commitment to Privacy

ONE E-Waste Emporia considers personal data privacy an ethical obligation grounded in the unchanged agape teachings of Yeshua ben Yosef. We treat your personal records, photos, communications, and identity with absolute sanctity. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal information under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact & Shipping Data: Full name, physical mailing address, email address, and phone number required to issue USPS/UPS shipping labels and communicate status.
  • Work Order Details: Device serial numbers, hardware models, passwords/PINs (when voluntarily provided solely for diagnostics), and disposition selections.
  • Billing Information: Payment processing is handled exclusively through Stripe. ONE Church never receives, views, or stores raw credit card numbers or banking secrets.

3. Handling of Customer Data on Storage Media

Hard drives, SSDs, flash memory, and smartphones often contain deeply private personal files. Our handling protocol includes:

  • Hardware Write-Blockers: Diagnostic imaging and data recovery workflows utilize physical or software write-blockers that prevent any data alteration or malware transfer.
  • NIST SP 800-88 Sanitization: Devices designated for wiping or donation undergo certified multi-pass overwrites or cryptographic erasure. A digital Certificate of Data Sanitization is issued to the owner.
  • Physical Security: All devices awaiting service or teardown are secured in access-controlled, video-monitored church annex storage.

4. Data Retention and Erasure Requests

Customer contact records and work orders are retained for five (5) years for non-profit auditing, tax compliance, and warranty records. Customers may request deletion of their contact profile at any time by emailing privacy@onechurch.global.
