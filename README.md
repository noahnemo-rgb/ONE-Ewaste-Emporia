## Mission & Agape Foundation

In the unchanged *agape* teachings of Yeshua ben Yosef, no person and no part of creation is disposable. Modern society routinely casts aside both obsolete electronics and human beings facing systemic marginalization — particularly individuals with disabilities and men and women returning home from incarceration.

**ONE E-Waste Emporia** operates as a restorative social enterprise and church ministry. We bridge ecological stewardship with human redemption: every discarded device entrusted to our mail-in service becomes a vehicle for technical training, career re-entry, and community renewal.

## The Four Core Operational Pillars

### 1. The Vocational Rehabilitation Guild
- **Fair-Chance Re-Entry Training:** Partnering with correctional facilities, transitional houses, and probation networks to offer paid apprenticeships for individuals rebuilding their lives after prison sentences.
- **Adaptive Workstations for the Disabled:** Ergonomic, ADA-compliant diagnostic benches, custom tactile and visual magnification tools, and supportive pacing tailored for neurodivergent and physically disabled technicians.
- **Certifications & Living Wages:** Apprentices earn living wages while mastering hardware diagnostics, micro-soldering, data sanitization, and preparing for industry credentials (e.g., CompTIA A+).

### 2. The Data Sanctuary (Data Recovery & Privacy)
- Non-invasive, bit-level forensic recovery using hardware write-blockers to extract family memories, photos, and historical records from damaged or unbootable drives.
- NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 certified cryptographic drive sanitization with cryptographically signed certificates of data destruction.

### 3. Upcycling & Digital Inclusion
- Viable laptops and desktops are inspected, ultrasonic-cleaned, upgraded with high-speed SSDs and RAM, and loaded with privacy-preserving open-source operating systems.
- Fully refurbished computers are gifted through church grants to low-income students, seniors, and non-profit community programs.

### 4. Sacred Earth Recycling (Zero Landfill)
- Complete manual de-manufacturing of non-viable electronics into pure commodity streams (aluminum, copper, PCB gold traces, recyclable plastics).
- Downstream processing partnered exclusively with R2v3 and e-Stewards certified facilities to ensure zero toxic waste is landfilled or exported.

## Getting Started

### Local Preview

Clone the repository and open `index.html` in your web browser:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/noahnemo-rgb/ONE-Ewaste-Emporia.git
cd ONE-Ewaste-Emporia
open index.html # On macOS, or double click index.html
```

### Deploying to Vercel (Recommended)

1. Connect your GitHub repository to Vercel at [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Select `noahnemo-rgb/ONE-Ewaste-Emporia`.
3. Keep default settings (Framework: *Other*, Root Directory: `./`).
4. Click **Deploy**. Vercel will deploy your site globally with automatic SSL.

### Deploying to GitHub Pages

1. In your GitHub repository, navigate to **Settings** > **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, select `Deploy from a branch`.
3. Choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
4. Your site will be live at `https://noahnemo-rgb.github.io/ONE-Ewaste-Emporia/`.
